Privacy Policy

What Guftugo collects, how it uses your information, and the choices available to you.

Effective: October 13, 2026Last updated: October 13, 2026
◆

Guftugo is an AI assistant. To provide chat history, sign-in, and document features, we process some personal information and the content you choose to submit. This page explains what we handle and why.

01

Information we collect

Account and sign-in information

  • Basic account details: your name, email address, account creation/update timestamps, and preferred response language.
  • Email/password accounts: a password hash is stored for sign-in; the backend is designed not to store your password in plain text.
  • Google sign-in: when you choose Continue with Google, Firebase Authentication authenticates you. Guftugo receives verified identity information such as your name, email address, and Firebase user identifier to create or connect your Guftugo account.

Chats and documents

  • Conversation data: chat titles, messages you send, AI responses, message timestamps, language preference, attached document references, and any returned web-source details.
  • Uploaded documents: file name, type, MIME type, size, extracted text, character count, a SHA-256 file hash, and upload timestamps. The backend accepts supported text files and text-based PDFs up to 5 MB; extracted text has a size limit. Scanned/image-only PDFs may not be readable.
  • Usage and diagnostics: the AI provider/model used, token counts (which may be estimated), response latency, success/failure status, fallback status, error codes, request identifiers, and service logs.
  • Session security metadata: refresh-token hashes, expiry/revocation timestamps, browser user-agent, and IP address associated with a session.
02

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, authenticate sign-ins, refresh sessions, and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To store and display conversation history and documents and provide language and account features.
  • To send the relevant prompt, recent conversation context, and any selected document excerpts to the configured AI service so it can generate a response.
  • To measure service usage, troubleshoot errors, monitor provider reliability, and protect the service against abuse.
  • To respond to support, privacy, or account-related requests.

Guftugo is an AI product and responses can be inaccurate. Avoid submitting passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, confidential business information, or other sensitive data unless you have carefully considered the risks.

04

Storage and retention

  • Chats: conversation history is stored in the database until you delete that conversation or request account/data deletion.
  • Uploaded documents: documents remain saved until you delete them or request their removal. Deleting a document removes its stored document record; prior chat messages may still retain a reference to that document.
  • Session records: refresh sessions have an expiry and revoked sessions are marked. Expired records may be removed by database expiry cleanup.
  • Operational telemetry: provider-health events are configured to expire after about 90 days. Usage logs do not currently have an automatic expiry configured, so they may remain until manually removed as part of maintenance or a data request.

The browser stores the app's access and refresh tokens in local storage so your session can persist between visits; signing out clears the locally stored session tokens. The interface also saves your appearance preference in local storage.

Deletion from the live database may not immediately remove copies in backups or logs where those exist. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for service operation, security, and legitimate recordkeeping, subject to applicable law.

05

Your choices and requests

  • You can delete individual conversations and uploaded documents using the controls provided in Guftugo.
  • You can sign out to end your current session.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of account-related information by emailing razazain861@gmail.com. Include the account email you used with Guftugo; never email passwords or private authentication keys.
  • The current app does not provide a self-service delete-account button. Contact us to request account deletion and we will review the request and explain any data that must be retained.
06

Security and changes

Application safeguards include password hashing, signed access/refresh tokens, token rotation, and access controls on user-owned conversations and documents. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong password and protect access to your device and email account.

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use of Guftugo.

07

Children’s privacy

Guftugo is a general-purpose assistant and is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Guftugo, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.
